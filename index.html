How to Play

Breakfast Pieces

Swipe or tap adjacent pieces to match 3 or more before running out of moves.

Special Boosters

  • Match 4 in a line: Creates a Striped Piece that blasts a full row or column.
  • Match L or T shape: Creates a Wrapped Piece that blasts a 3×3 area.
  • Match 5 in a line: Creates a Super Espresso Bomb — swap with any piece to clear all of that type!
  • Combine any 2 specials for massive chain combos!

Every 100 Levels ☕

When you beat Level 100 (and 200, 300…), you’ll be asked: “Did you have your coffee today?” Say Yes to unlock the next 100 levels, or No to return to Level 1 with freshly reshuffled levels!
